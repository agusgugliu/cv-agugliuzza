Just closed out the Tech Lab challenge at IE Business School's International MBA, five weeks building with Microsoft as our corporate partner.

The problem: olive oil mills lose real money in a 90-minute window during milling, because grade gets set (and destroyed) faster than anyone can actually measure it.

What we built: Almazara Copilot, an agent system that predicts grade at intake, tells the mill master how to adjust milling in real time, keeps an eye on quality through the whole process, and spits out the compliance paperwork the mills already have to file anyway.

Talked to mill directors and quality managers in Jaén, ran the numbers down to the co-op level, and got our pitch torn apart more than once by our mentors Silvia Rodenas Vaquero (Microsoft) and Mario Trueba Cantero (KPMG), in a good way. They didn't let us skate on the hard questions.

Really proud of what six of us pulled off in five weeks.
