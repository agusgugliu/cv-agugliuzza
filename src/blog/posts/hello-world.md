I'm adding a blog to this site. Not a content calendar, not a newsletter —
just a place to write down things I'm thinking about when I want to think
about them out loud: AI tools, the MBA, Argentina, whatever I end up posting
on LinkedIn and want a permanent home for.

Posts here are plain markdown files in the repo. No CMS, no database — I
write a file, push, it's live.
